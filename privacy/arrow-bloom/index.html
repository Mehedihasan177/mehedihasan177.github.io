Arrow Bloom – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how the Arrow Bloom mobile game ("the app", "we") handles information. We keep it simple: we do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels cleared, coins, skins, settings such as sound and vibration, and in-progress levels) is saved locally on your device. We do not receive this data and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob (banner, full-screen, app-open and rewarded video ads). To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use certain data from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. This may be used for personalised or non-personalised ads, depending on your device settings and the region you are in.

  • Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Rewarded ads

Some features (for example getting an extra life or doubling coins) are optional and are unlocked by watching a rewarded video ad. Watching is always your choice.

Ratings

If you choose to rate the app, you are taken to the Google Play Store, which is governed by Google's own privacy policy. We do not receive your Google account details.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. The only third party that receives data through the app is Google (AdMob), as described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Because your game data stays on your device and we operate no user accounts or servers for it, we hold no personal data about you.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this page, with the effective date shown above.
